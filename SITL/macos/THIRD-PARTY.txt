This package includes AM32 firmware and AM32 bootloader SITL executables
and the ESCSim Python GUI. Source:
https://github.com/am32-firmware/ESCSim
https://github.com/am32-firmware/AM32
https://github.com/am32-firmware/AM32-bootloader

The GUI includes Python, PySide6/Qt, PyQtGraph, NumPy, DroneCAN, pymavlink,
pyserial and their dependencies, packaged with PyInstaller. Their licences
and copyright notices remain in the application bundle where supplied by
the distributions. PySide6 and Qt are used under their LGPL terms; a copy
of LGPLv3 is provided as LGPL-3.0.txt. They are dynamically loaded libraries
inside the .app and may be replaced with compatible modified versions;
modifying the app requires signing the modified bundle again.
